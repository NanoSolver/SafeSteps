# SafeStride launch film

SafeStride is a college prototype that helps people plan a walk, view community reports, and share a trusted-contact arrival check-in. The hero copy in the source app is: “The way there can feel better.” The video is aimed at classmates and a project-review audience.

## Creative direction

Warm, polished, and direct. Open with the real product line, then show the route planner, a community hazard card, and the check-in changing to “Arrived.” Keep the supplied mint, forest-green, ink, white-card, and blue-route palette, plus the app’s Playfair Display / DM Sans typography. Source styles are adapted into static scene markup because the app is a vanilla JavaScript single-page app; no live records or accounts are needed for the video.

Use fictional `Demo Walker` data and label the map path/report as illustrative. Avoid invented safety claims, verified street conditions, real route results, or customer records. The project is Bengaluru-focused and the area survey is planned, not complete.

## Storyboard (22s, 30fps, landscape)

| Time | Scene | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3.5s | “The way there can feel better.” Route line lands at a home marker. | Hook |
| 3.25–9.2s | Planner labels: Cubbon Park → MG Road Metro. Fastest and Alternate cards reveal. No fabricated route times or distances; map is explicitly illustrative. | Plan a walk |
| 9.2–13.45s | Community report card: Broken streetlight, marked Demo report. | See a report |
| 13.45–17.47s | Trusted-contact view shows Walk in progress with Demo Walker and sample ETA. | Check in |
| 17.47–18.56s | Contact status changes to Arrived. All personal data is marked demo. | Arrival |
| 18.56–22s | SafeStride, public URL, “Bengaluru college project · Area survey planned.” | Close |

## Audio and share line

Use the already available bundled 109.96 BPM music cue. Make the arrival flip and closing brand land on the planned musical accents; keep soft UI effects under the music. No narration. Caption: “Plan a walk, see community reports, and let someone know when you get home. SafeStride is our Bengaluru college project; an area survey is planned.”
