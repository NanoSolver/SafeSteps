# SafeSteps Hyperframes composition brief

Render a 22-second polished 1920×1080 landscape MP4 at 30 fps. Use brag-plan.md as the storyboard. Source files are app.js, street-map.js, check-in.js, styles.css and README.md in the project root. Show recreated working UI with staged public landmarks and fictional records, keeping its SafeStride branding while the film’s product title is SafeSteps.

LOOK — #e3f1e9 paper-like mint ground, #1b2a23 ink, #2c624b forest, #2964df route, Playfair Display 700 at 84–130px and Inter 400/700 at 30–42px. White UI cards have 18px corners and 2px #d8e0db borders. Main stage inset 120px. Text at least 30px. Animated route traces, one deliberate pin drop, check-in state flip. No looping title motion.

BANNED — unverified safety scores, crime-risk claims, automated danger detection, private tokens, real database rows, generic SaaS promises, stock people, floating text loops.

Use five scene sub-compositions, with paused seekable timelines and local assets. Music is bundled vol. 12 with two low-risk click/drop cues and a soft reveal cue. No narration. Copy audio into the film assets directory. Contrast and layout must pass Hyperframes check. Sample every scene before rendering. Render local delivery MP4, extract the strongest settled check-in frame as brag.jpg and replace the first video frame with that poster.

The new CLI distribution has no separate hyperframes-registry skill. Its current catalog command was searched for title effects. A custom source-UI recreation fits this storyboard better than those title blocks.
