# SafeSteps launch film

SafeSteps helps people plan walks in Bengaluru, see community hazards and share an arrival check-in. The working interface currently calls the product SafeStride. Use SafeSteps in launch copy and retain SafeStride inside the reproduced UI.

## Planning rubric

1. App — street directions, reports, havens and trusted-contact check-ins.
2. Strongest line — The way there can feel better.
3. Hook — Going home? A route draws toward a home pin beside the question.
4. Actual UI — Plan a walk sidebar, Fastest/Alternate route cards, hazard report popup, and the check-in state changing to Arrived.
5. Duration — 22 seconds, landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps.
6. Tone — polished, warm campus-project product film with decisive interactions.
7. Audio — bundled Business Moves vol. 12 at 0.30, a soft reveal and two UI accents, no narration.
8. Share caption — Built SafeSteps for the walk home. Compare walking routes, flag street hazards, and share an arrival check-in with someone you trust. Bengaluru pilot, with our area survey still ahead.
9. User flow — choose start/destination, compare a walk, see a report, share check-in, tap I’m home.

## Visual identity

Mint #e3f1e9, forest #2c624b, ink #1b2a23, white cards and blue route #2964df. Playfair Display headings, Inter for UI as the bundled substitute for DM Sans. Wide whitespace, 18px card corners, 2px borders. Use the actual app labels. All route figures and contact details are staged demonstration records. The map is an illustrative UI recreation rather than a navigation recording.

3.3–9.3 — Plan the walk. Actual Plan a walk UI recreated with Cubbon Park and MG Road. Two illustrative route cards show Fastest 21 min and Alternate 24 min. Route traces in as the selection settles. Header reads Pick your way home. A subtle click accents selection.

9.3–13.7 — Street reports. Same map framing. A hazard pin opens the Broken streetlight popup, labelled Demo report. Text reads See what’s been reported. Cards and popup move in once, then hold. A soft drop matches the pin.

18.6–22 — Outro. SafeSteps. The way there can feel better. Public URL safesteps-smoky.vercel.app and Bengaluru college project. Music fades over last 1.8 seconds. Final copy holds at least 2 seconds.
